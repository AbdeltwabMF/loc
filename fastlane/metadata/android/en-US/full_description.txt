Loc is a private, free and open-source arrival reminder for Android. Choose a destination and Loc alerts you when you enter its arrival radius.

Features:
<ul>
  <li>Multiple independently enabled reminders</li>
  <li>Configurable arrival radius from 100 m to 5 km</li>
  <li>Brief sound, vibration-only, or repeating alarm alerts</li>
  <li>Background tracking with a visible ongoing notification</li>
  <li>High-speed arrival-zone crossing detection</li>
  <li>Built-in OpenStreetMap picker and external map selection</li>
  <li>Shared location imports, manual coordinates, and pinned reminders</li>
  <li>Light, dark, and system themes</li>
</ul>

Reminders stay on your device. Loc has no account, advertising, analytics, or application backend. Update checks are manual and use GitHub Releases.
