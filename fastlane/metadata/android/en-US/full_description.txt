Loc is a free and open-source location reminder for Android. Set your destination. Loc alerts you as you get nearby.

Features:
<ul>
  <li>Multiple independently enabled reminders</li>
  <li>Configurable arrival radius from 20 m to 50 km</li>
  <li>Notification sound, vibration, or repeating alarm alerts</li>
  <li>Background tracking with a visible ongoing notification</li>
  <li>High-speed arrival-zone crossing detection</li>
  <li>Built-in OpenStreetMap picker and external map selection</li>
  <li>Shared location imports and manual coordinates</li>
  <li>Pinned reminders with quick long-press actions</li>
</ul>
