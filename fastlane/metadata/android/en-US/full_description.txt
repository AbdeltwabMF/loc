Loc is a private, free and open-source arrival reminder for Android. Choose a destination and Loc alerts you when you enter its arrival radius.

Features:
<ul>
  <li>Multiple independently enabled reminders</li>
  <li>Configurable arrival radius from 100 m to 5 km</li>
  <li>Brief notifications or repeating alarms per reminder</li>
  <li>Foreground tracking with screen-off support</li>
  <li>High-speed arrival-zone crossing detection</li>
  <li>OpenStreetMap map and Nominatim place search</li>
  <li>Manual coordinates and reusable saved places</li>
  <li>Light, dark, and system themes</li>
</ul>

Reminders and saved places stay on your device. Loc has no account, advertising, analytics, or application backend.
