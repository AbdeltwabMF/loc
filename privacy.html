LOC

Privacy

Loc has no accounts, ads, analytics, or backend server. Your reminders and saved places stay on your device.

Location

Loc uses precise location while active reminders are running, including in the background. Your location is compared with your destinations directly on your device. Loc does not send your movement history to a Loc server.

Map and search services

Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. When you search for a place, Loc sends your search text to Nominatim. When Loc looks up a place from map coordinates, those coordinates are sent to Nominatim.

Running Diagnostics also contacts both services to check whether they are available. No location, search, or reminder content is included in those checks.

These services receive standard network information such as your IP address and are governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policies.

OpenStreetMap privacy policy

Data stored on your device

Reminder names, notes, coordinates, saved places, arrival settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

You can delete individual items in Loc. To remove all locally stored Loc data, clear the app's storage in Android settings.

Permissions

Loc requests location, background location, and notification access so reminders can continue working when the screen is off and alert you when you reach your destination. Android lets you review or revoke these permissions at any time.

Questions

Questions about privacy? Review the source code or open an issue on GitHub.
